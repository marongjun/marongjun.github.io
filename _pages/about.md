I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [HASP Lab](https://hasp-lab.github.io/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, VRAIN, Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV), Spain, working with Professor [Jose Such](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=xNr_IMUAAAAJ){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Aalto University, Finland, under the supervision of Associate Professor [Janne Lindqvist](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=rOW28UoAAAAJ){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. Before that, I completed my M.Sc. through the EIT Joint Master Program at Aalto University, Finland, and the University of Twente, the Netherlands.

I study **usable security and privacy in human–AI interaction**. My research follows three interconnected directions:

- **Technology Appropriation:** I study how people adopt, adapt, and integrate technologies, from web browsers to emerging AI systems, into everyday life, and how to support their responsible appropriation.
- **Ecosystem Perspectives on Privacy:** I examine how interconnected stakeholders, including users, creators, providers, and third parties, shape privacy experiences and management strategies in human–AI interaction.
- **Human–AI Relationships and Relational Privacy:** I explore how AI evolves from a tool into a relational partner, particularly in romantic relationships, and how these dynamics call for a more relational and evolving understanding of privacy.

My research has been published in leading HCI conferences and journals, including **ACM CHI** and **ACM CSCW**. You can find a selection of my publications below.
